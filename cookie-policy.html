About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how ABS Training, part of the American Bureau of Shipping (“ABS”, “we”, “us”), uses cookies and similar technologies on abs-training.com, and how you can control them. It should be read together with the ABS Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

Course registration, sign-in and payment take place on the ABS Training portal at portal.abs-training.com, which is provided by our learning platform partner and uses its own cookies. This policy covers abs-training.com only.

Your cookie choices

You can accept or reject analytics and advertising cookies at any time. Changes apply right away, and rejecting deletes the cookies those services have already set on this site.

{% endif %}

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in the same way. They let a site remember information between pages and visits. In this policy, “cookies” includes these similar technologies.

First-party cookies are set by abs-training.com. Third-party cookies are set by another company’s domain, such as google.com.

Cookies we use

We group cookies into three categories. Only strictly necessary storage is used without your choice where consent is required.

Needed for the site to work as you expect. These can’t be switched off in our cookie settings.

How your choices work

  • When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all, reject all or customize analytics and advertising cookies.
  • If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies stay off unless you accept them.
  • If you visit from elsewhere, analytics and advertising cookies are on until you make a choice. You can reject them at any time.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, advertising cookies are off by default.
  • When analytics or advertising is off, Google may still receive basic information without cookies, such as that a page was viewed, so it can produce aggregated, anonymous reports. These signals don’t identify you and don’t store anything on your device.
  • Rejecting a category deletes the cookies that category has set on abs-training.com.

Other services on this site

These services don’t set cookies on abs-training.com, but your browser connects to them to load parts of the page, which shares your IP address and browser details with the provider:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): text fonts and icons.
  • Tailwind CSS (cdn.tailwindcss.com): page styling.
  • ABS (ww2.eagle.org) and Unsplash (images.unsplash.com): images.
  • ABS Training portal (portal.abs-training.com): upcoming class dates shown on course pages.

Links to the ABS Training portal, eagle.org and LinkedIn take you to sites with their own cookie and privacy policies.

Managing cookies

  • {% if tracking_on %}Use Cookie Settings in the footer of any page, or the button at the top of this page.{% else %}This site currently sets no analytics or advertising cookies.{% endif %}

  • Learn how Google uses data in How Google uses information from sites that use its services (opens in a new tab).
