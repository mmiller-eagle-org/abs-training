This three-day course equips maritime professionals with the instructional and assessment skills needed to deliver effective training that meets Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW) and United States Coast Guard (USCG) requirements. Participants learn to plan outcome-based programs, apply adult learning principles and evaluate trainee competency through classroom instruction, workshops and a live teaching presentation.
