The International Safety Management (ISM) Code recommends that each company assign a designated person ashore (DPA) and DPA Assistant to monitor the safety and pollution prevention aspects of each ship's operation. This two-day course builds an understanding of the authority and liability issues of the DPA role, with particular attention to the safety management system, planned maintenance system, vessel response plan and the Vessel General Permit. Reference is made to current International Maritime Organization (IMO) regulations, and case studies provide practical approaches for avoiding undesirable outcomes. This course satisfies the requirements for element 6 of the Designated Person Ashore standard.
