Many incidents, hazardous occurrences and near misses aboard maritime assets stem from human error and can result in considerable loss. This two-day course shows participants how to identify and understand contributory factors and root causes so preventive measures can be put in place. Using the ABS Guidance Notes on the Investigation of Marine Incidents, participants apply the ABS Marine Root Cause Analysis Technique (MaRCAT) through a series of group workshops.
