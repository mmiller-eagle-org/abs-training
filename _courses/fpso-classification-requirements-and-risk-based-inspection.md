This four-day course builds a practical understanding of the classification requirements for floating production, storage and offloading units (FPSOs) and floating storage units (FSOs). Participants learn how ABS Rules, Guides, standards and regulations are applied across the design, conversion, installation and operation of F(P)SO projects to better evaluate technical requirements, reduce project risk and support sound classification decisions.
