This two-day course explains the ship loading and discharging operations cycle from a gas-free condition until a cargo change is planned, along with an overview of the systems involved in cargo handling operations. Gas carriers use cargo handling systems designed for liquefied gases in bulk, including discharging and control systems featuring main cargo pumps, booster pumps, cargo heaters, level gauging and a high-level alarm system.
